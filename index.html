Frequently asked questions

What ages do you coach? +
We coach kids aged 4 to 12, grouped by stage — Mini Kickers (4–6), Skill Builders (7–9) and Game Ready (10–12).
Do you offer a free trial? +
Yes! Your child's first session is completely free with no commitment. Just book through the enquiry form and come along.
What should my child wear and bring? +
Comfortable sportswear, runners or boots, shin pads, a drink bottle and a hat. We supply the balls and equipment.
What happens if it rains? +
If a session is cancelled for weather we'll let you know as early as we can and reschedule or credit the session.
Where are sessions held? +
At parks and grounds across Sydney metro and regional NSW — and we can travel to your local park, school or club. See Locations above.

Do you provide the equipment? +
